How It Works

From raw data to a decision.

Four stages. Each one is checked before the next one runs.

01

Data

We assemble three public series per ZIP: Zillow ZHVI, Zillow ZORI, and Census ACS 5-year estimates. The work is in the joins and the cleaning, not the downloading; every series is aligned to the same ZIP boundaries and the same year before anything is scored.

02

Model

The model learns which combinations of conditions preceded outperformance in past cycles. It is validated walk-forward: for every year we test, it only sees data that existed before that year, and outcomes are embargoed until the test is scored. The features and the validation record are ours; the public data is not the product.

03

Analytics

Raw model output is converted to a 0-100 score, a tier, and the specific conditions that drove the score for that ZIP. The thresholds have not changed since 2026-06-07.

04

Report

Packaged into a decision-ready Market Signal Report: the answer first, the analytics behind it.
