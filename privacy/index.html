Privacy

What we collect, and what we don't.

Effective 2026-09-24. This covers spotdg.com. The dashboard at app.spotdg.com has its own sign-in and is currently limited to the SpotWise team.

01

Analytics without cookies.

We measure site traffic with Plausible, which counts page views in aggregate and does not use cookies, does not store your IP address, and does not follow you to other sites. We see which pages are read and roughly where visits come from. We cannot see who you are.

02

The one form on this site goes to the founders.

When you request a report through the Founding Program form, you give us your name, your email, optionally your company, and the market you want scored. The form is delivered by Formspree, a form-handling service, and lands in the founders' inboxes. We use it to build your report and to reach you about it, and for nothing else. We keep it for as long as we are working with you, and we delete it when you ask.

03

Hosting and fonts.

The site is served by Netlify, which keeps standard server logs, and the typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, so those two providers see the request your browser makes for the page and for the fonts. Neither is used by us to identify or track you.

04

Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared for advertising.

We do not sell, rent, or trade anything you give us. We do not run advertising trackers or pixels on this site. If that ever changes, this page changes first and the effective date above moves.

05

Ask us anything about your data.

To see what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, email any founder at the addresses in the footer. We answer from the same inboxes the form goes to. This site is not directed at anyone under 18.

That is the whole policy.
