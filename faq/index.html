FAQ

Common questions.

What we hear most from people evaluating SpotWise. Don't see yours? Contact us directly.

01What is a Market Signal Report?

A decision-ready report scoring a zip code or market on appreciation momentum, backed by our walk-forward validated model: built for investors, lenders, and developers evaluating where to focus next.

02How is the momentum score calculated?

We aggregate public real estate, economic, and demographic data into a proprietary model, walk-forward validated with a strict outcome embargo: trained only on data available before each prediction, with outcome labels withheld until fully realized, then tested against real outcomes over five-year holding periods.

03Which markets do you cover?

The NJ/NY/PA tri-state region, 1,213 zip codes.

04How often is the data updated?

Core scores refresh monthly.

05Is this financial or investment advice?

No. SpotWise provides market intelligence to support your own due diligence. It is not financial or investment advice, and past performance does not guarantee future results.

06What is the Founding Market Intelligence Program?

The Founding Market Intelligence Program gives early participants a complimentary Market Signal Report for a market they choose, with a walkthrough from the founders. In return, we ask for a short feedback session so we can improve the report.

07Do you offer a subscription, or is it per-report?

Yes. SpotWise runs on a subscription model. Each tier includes a monthly allotment of Market Signal Reports with the ability to add more as needed; see Plans for what's included in each tier.
