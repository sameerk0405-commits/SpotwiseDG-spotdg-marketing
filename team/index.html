Our Story

Three founders, one shared frustration.

SpotWise started with Sameer at Rutgers, Joe at Emory, and Andrew at Loyola. They were comparing notes on how real estate investment decisions actually get made. Too often, it wasn't data. It was a broker's gut feeling, or a comp sheet that was already a year stale.

We thought investors, lenders, and developers deserved better than a rearview mirror. So we built the tool we wished existed: a walk-forward validated model that tells you where a neighborhood is headed, not just where it's been. It gives a forward-looking edge instead of a guess.

Our mission

We turn real estate appreciation into something you can see coming, not something you find out about after it's priced in.

Our vision

To be the standard first check before any real estate capital moves in the Northeast, and then everywhere else.

Shipped: 1,213 ZIPs scored · eight scoring batches · a walk-forward validation record we publish
