Plans

Four ways to engage.

SpotWise Analyst

For solo deal flow
What's included

Monthly access to a set number of zip-level Market Signal Reports, with the ability to add more as needed. Each report includes the momentum score, tier classification, top signals, and investor ratios for a single zip or market of your choosing.

Who it's for

Independent flippers and local wholesalers who move on one opportunity at a time and want a fast, data-backed read on a zip before committing capital or a contract.

SpotWise Pro

For active deal teams
What's included

A larger monthly allotment of Market Signal Reports, plus side-by-side comparison and ranking across the geographies you're evaluating, with the ability to add more as your pipeline grows.

Who it's for

Investment syndicates and professional flippers actively sourcing and underwriting several opportunities in parallel need the numbers ranked next to each other, not read one report at a time.

SpotWise Enterprise

For lending & fund operations
What's included

A monthly report allotment sized for underwriting volume, team-wide access so your whole lending or fund operation works from the same signal, and the ability to add capacity as deal flow picks up.

Who it's for

Regional hard money lenders and micro-funds that need to underwrite collateral across a steady flow of deals, not a single transaction, want momentum data built into how their team evaluates risk.

SpotWise Lender Scale

For institutional underwriting
What's included

Unlimited query access for underwriting at scale, so momentum data sits inside your underwriting workflow without a monthly ceiling, built for teams evaluating collateral continuously across a large book.

Who it's for

Institutional fund underwriters and large credit pools where deal volume is constant and a capped monthly allotment would be a bottleneck, not a convenience.
